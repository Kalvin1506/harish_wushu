About the workshop

Stop guessing.
Start understanding.

Most people don't fail at fitness because of a lack of effort—they fail because of confusing, conflicting information. This workshop gives you a clear, reliable foundation in one focused live session.

✦

What it’s about

A structured, single-day deep dive into the fundamentals of fitness—so you understand the why behind every workout and meal.

◎

Who should attend

Beginners, regular gym-goers who’ve hit a plateau, and anyone who wants to improve their health with clarity.

◉

What you’ll learn

12 practical topics of roughly 25 minutes each, giving you knowledge you can apply from the very next day.

≋

Why it’s different

Live, focused and structured—learn directly from Harish instead of piecing together scattered advice.
